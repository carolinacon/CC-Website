What is CarolinaCon?

CarolinaCon is a hacking conference put together annually since 2005. It's a place for both local and global communities to learn more about technology, information/network/computer security, and information rights.

Each year hackers, hobbyists, and newbies come out of the shadows to:

  • 👋   Hangout with other techies
  • 🧑‍💻   Develop or sharpen skills
  • 🚩   Compete in the CTF
  • 📚   Share knowledge, stories, resources
  • 🍻   And sometimes meetup for a beer

Who runs CarolinaCon?

CarolinaCon is put together and run by a staff of volunteers located in both Charlotte and Raleigh NC. The current staff is a group of past 49th Security Division members (a student hacking club from UNC Charlotte).

What kind of events are there?

CarolinaCon usually features educational talks, demos, workshops, hacking challenges, a CTF, and other in-person events.

For CarolinaCons hosted online, we place a larger emphasis on our CTF and livestreamed talks, as those events translate well to an online format. Our online conferences may still have some in-person events or meetups, so keep an eye out for schedule updates!

Does CarolinaCon cost money?

While we did charge an admission fee for our in-person conferences, our online conferences are free!

Because we are an all-volunteer staff, our main costs for the con revolve around fees paid to the hotel for hosting. With an online con, our costs are greatly reduced and we can run this con from saved funds and donations.

Do you take sponsors or donations?

Short answer:   ✅ Donations yes!   ❌ Sponsors no

Sponsors Capitalism and philanthropic knowledge-sharing don't mix in our opinion. When the con is held in person we keep our admission price to the bare minimum to cover our venue and equipment expenses. All of our staff are volunteers who generously donate their time and energy. All of our presenters generously donate their time and talent. We typically sell a merch bundle to get a shirt, electronic badge, and sticker, because people enjoy them. These sales rarely make money.

Donations Well that's a different story. We will gladly accept donations from anyone who wants to contribute. At CarolinaCon, we pride ourselves on not charging a lot for admission so we don't have a lot to spend on giveaways (we manage though). We can always use prizes for Hacker Trivia and various other contests that we run. So if you want to donate an actual prize, rather than cash, just let us know by sending an email to: info (at) carolinacon.org
